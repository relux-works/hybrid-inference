## Admission and exclusions

A candidate must declare context >=262144 for Qwen, or >=1000000 for GLM, and a nonnegative cache-read price. Zero cache-read price counts as declared caching; absent is unknown. Explicit cache-write pricing is retained; lack of implicit caching uses an ephemeral breakpoint. Exact endpoint tag is mandatory. Exclusions and reasons are generated mechanically from the frozen list, never from observed cost or performance. Low uptime endpoints remain in the experiment and fail the same admission criterion as everyone else.

Baseline admission requires known measured cache counters and at least one positive cached-token observation, own observed availability >=95%, and daily uptime >=95% at each of at least three distinct sampled timestamps. All planned attempts must have been recorded for that endpoint; a budget-truncated endpoint is pending. Daily uptime uses provider rolling 1-day statistics, sampled before every call, with 5-minute and 30-minute fields retained. Repeated daily windows overlap: neither those samples nor a few hours of our requests establishes long-term availability. Missing daily statistics or too few samples leaves admission pending. Observed availability counts HTTP errors and pinning/usage/depth failures as failed attempts; budget refusals before POST are not attempts.
